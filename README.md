# 📊 HR Analytics Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing HR data to uncover workforce trends, attrition patterns, training effectiveness, and training cost efficiency. The analysis was performed using Python to support data-driven HR decision-making and business optimization.

The dataset includes employee demographics, job details, employment status, training programs, and cost information.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand workforce distribution and diversity
- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Evaluate training effectiveness
- Optimize training costs
- Generate actionable HR insights and recommendations

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains information related to:
- Employee demographics (Age, Gender, Marital Status)
- Job details (Department, Business Unit, Job Title)
- Employment status and tenure
- Training programs, outcomes, duration, and cost

---

## 🔍 Analysis Tasks Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Handled missing values and duplicates
- Converted date columns to proper datetime format
- Created new features such as Age and Tenure
- Grouped Age and Tenure into meaningful categories

### 2. Workforce Distribution Analysis
- Employee distribution by department
- Employee distribution by business unit
- Top job roles by employee count

### 3. Workforce Diversity Analysis
- Gender distribution
- Employment type distribution
- Pay zone distribution

### 4. Employment Status & Attrition Analysis
- Active vs terminated employee analysis
- Overall attrition rate calculation
- Attrition analysis by department and tenure group

### 5. Training Effectiveness Analysis
- Training outcome distribution
- Training effectiveness by training type
- Average training duration and cost by outcome

### 6. Training Cost Optimization
- Total training expenditure
- Training cost by department and training type
- Identification of high-cost, low-impact training programs

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- High attrition observed among employees with 0–1 year tenure
- Certain departments show higher attrition risk
- Internal training programs have higher completion rates
- High training cost does not always ensure better outcomes
- Training effectiveness improves with employee experience

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve onboarding and first-year employee engagement
- Implement department-specific retention strategies
- Optimize training budgets by focusing on high-impact programs
- Expand internal training initiatives
- Customize training programs based on employee tenure
- Strengthen diversity and inclusion initiatives
- Use HR dashboards for continuous monitoring

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how HR analytics can help organizations improve employee retention, optimize training investments, and make informed workforce decisions using data.
